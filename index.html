Wordforge

Same idea as that word game you know. More letters, more modes, more pressure.

Mode

Rounds

How it works
  1. Every round picks a secret word — anywhere from 4 to 7 letters, so don't assume it's always five.
  2. Most rounds must start with the last letter of the previous round's word. Watch the chain badge.
  3. Type guesses on the on-screen keyboard or your own. Tiles show hit (right spot), present (wrong spot) or miss — and any letter you've used that isn't in the word greys out on the keyboard, so you never waste a guess retyping it.
  4. Every round has a 5-minute clock. Run out, and the round ends there — revealed, unsolved, for whoever hadn't gotten it yet.
  5. Solo: no lobby, no code — just you against the word, same guess budget as a duel. Good for warming up or chasing a personal best.
  6. PvP Duel: you and a rival race the same word at the same time. You only ever see your own board — the "ghost" bar shows how many guesses your rival has burned, nothing more. Whoever solves it first wins the round, however many guesses it took.
  7. Co-op: the whole room shares one board and one guess budget (word length + 3, no bigger a team just because you invited more people). Anyone can drop the next guess — coordinate or waste attempts.
  8. Points build across the whole match. After the last round, whoever — or whichever team — has the most wins.
