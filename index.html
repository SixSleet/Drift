Drift: Chaos Mode

Nudge the ball. Lose the ball. Guess where it went.

Rounds

How it works
  1. A ball launches into a bumper arena. Every 3rd round, two or three at once.
  2. You get one click all round — it shoves the nearest ball away from where you clicked.
  3. Watch for round modifiers: gravity wells, turbo speed, drifting walls, a shrinking arena, a blinking ball.
  4. Tap 1, 2 or 3 to wager how sure you are. Nobody sees your wager.
  5. Near the end the balls go dark and keep moving. Then everything freezes.
  6. You have 3 seconds to click where you think your ball ended up.
  7. Points scale with how close you were, times your wager. Land 3 close guesses in a row for a multiplier.
