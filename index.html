How it works
  1. Pick the word length before you create the room — 4, 5, 6, 7, or Mixed (4 or 5, re-rolled every round). It's fixed for the whole match, and anyone who joins inherits it.
  2. Most rounds must start with the last letter of the previous round's word. Watch the chain badge.
  3. Type guesses on your own keyboard — the one on the desk in front of you. There's nothing on screen to click. Each letter pops in as you type it. Tiles flip to show hit (right spot), present (wrong spot) or miss, and a guess that lands zero hits gets its own shake and sting. Any letter you've used that isn't in the word greys out in the legend below the board, so you never waste a guess retyping it.
  4. Every round has a 5-minute clock, ticking audibly in the last 10 seconds. Run out, and the round ends there — revealed, unsolved, for whoever hadn't gotten it yet.
  5. Round-start events: about 3 rounds in 5 draw one, announced across the whole screen for the full 5-second countdown so nobody misses it. Some move a number — 💰 double points, ⚡ a 90-second blitz, and a rare 🎰 Jackpot (extra guess AND double points, with confetti and a slot-machine fanfare). The rest change the rules: 🙈 blackout stops the legend tracking what you've ruled out, 🔢 Cipher takes the colours away and tells you only how many hits and presents you scored, 🔒 Lockdown makes every guess reuse the letters you've confirmed, 🫥 Fading Ink drains the colour off each row seconds after it lands, 🚫 Banned Letter outlaws a letter for the round, 🩸 Sudden Death ends the round on a guess that scores nothing at all, and 🎲 Wager lets you stake your points on solving it.
  6. Mid-round modifiers: a round can spring a second modifier on you partway through instead of announcing it up front — 💰 double points from here, ⚡ the remaining clock halved, 🙈 the legend going dark, or (Co-op only) a 🔀 Letter Swap that makes two players' guesses trade tiles. These are global: everyone in the room gets the same one, at the same moment.
  7. Your room: meanwhile the place you're sitting in carries on without you — 🐈 a cat strolls across the desk right in front of the screen, a moth finds the lamp, the phone buzzes, the light stutters, rain starts at the window. These are yours alone: nobody else sees them, they change nothing about the game, and the only cost is that they're in your way. Click one to clear it early.
  8. Solo: no lobby, no code — just you against the word, same guess budget as a duel. Good for warming up or chasing a personal best.
  9. PvP Duel: you and a rival race the same word at the same time. You only ever see your own board — the "ghost" bar shows how many guesses your rival has burned, nothing more. Whoever solves it first wins the round, however many guesses it took.
  10. Co-op: the whole room shares one board and one guess budget (word length + 3, no bigger a team just because you invited more people). Anyone can drop the next guess — coordinate or waste attempts.
  11. Points build across the whole match. After the last round, whoever — or whichever team — has the most wins.
